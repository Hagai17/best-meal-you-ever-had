Entry for the make.fun bounty [The Best Meal You Ever Had](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=best-meal-you-ever-had).

`main.py` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My father split a durian on the porch with his cleaver because my mother had banned it from the house, and we ate it with our fingers until she smelled it on us and claimed the last piece herself.

The line was picked by duelling ten drafts against the competition's
published judge brief and the strongest lines already in the live field:
159 finished entries were read first-hand, and the field stampedes into
grandmother's rendang, 2 a.m. instant noodles and steam fogging glasses —
durian appears nowhere in it. The champion stays in that empty lane:
a named fruit, a place, a smell and a texture, and a closing turn — the
mother who banned it claims the last piece herself. The full draft list,
lane analysis and duel record are in [candidates.md](candidates.md).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt and prints the sentence
```
